Everything begins with change.

Humanity advances by resolving friction. Technology changes how people live, and changed lives create new friction and desire. New value emerges from that gap.

Young people are less bound by the existing order and often adopt change first. The future therefore appears first in their behavior.

Business is the act of reading change and owning the value it will create.

What matters is not what is large today, but what is changing.
